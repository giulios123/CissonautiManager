# Waitlist

If a slot is full you can join the waitlist: do it from the registration form (even with only full slots) or from your page, in the “Waitlist” section.

- **You pay nothing** until a place frees up.
- The list has a limit: if it is full you cannot join.
- You see your position, for example “number 1 on the list”.
- When a place frees up (a cancellation, a slot removed, more capacity) the first person on the list gets an email and the slot becomes theirs. If you already had a booking, the slot is added and the amounts are recalculated. If you didn't, a new one is created: if the required fee is positive, you have its hold period to pay it; with a zero fee, the place is already confirmed. Under the new rule, extras can be paid later.
- People invited by someone, with the link confirmed by the organizers, go ahead on the list.
- You can leave the list at any time. If you book the slot you were waiting for yourself, you leave the list automatically.

You are not notified if your position changes: we only write to you when it is your turn.
