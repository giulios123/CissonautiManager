# Activities

The organizers offer activities (hikes, workshops and more), free or paid. You find them under “Choose activities” inside your registration.

- You only see activities for the times when you are here: if you are missing the slot, add it first.
- Some have a limited number of places: when they run out the checkbox is locked and “full” appears.
- If the organizers collect payment, the price is added to the fee and you see a preview before saving.
- If you remove the slot of that time, the activity is dropped and the app lets you know.

Activities show start and end times. Activities paid directly to the provider show their cost, recipient and payment instructions before sign-up and in your summary; their cost is excluded from the camp fee.
