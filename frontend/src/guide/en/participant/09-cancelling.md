# Cancelling your registration

From your page press “Request cancellation” and confirm.

- Your places are released right away and cannot be recovered without signing up again.
- **If you hadn't paid anything**, the registration is simply cancelled.
- **If you had already paid**, the status becomes “Cancellation requested”. The organizers write to you about the refund and then mark it as “Refunded”: the app does not refund on its own.
- You also leave any waitlists you were on.

You get a confirmation email of the cancellation.
