# Contributions: what you bring to the camp

The camp lives on what people bring. Under “What you bring to the camp” you can propose:

- a **talk**,
- a **topic for the unconference**,
- a **skill to share**,
- a **skill you are looking for**, if you need someone who can do something.

You can add as many as you like, with a title and, if you want, a description. You can edit and remove them at any time while your registration is active. The organizers see them all in a dedicated view.

## Who sees them

Title and description are also visible to the other participants: do not write other people's names or contact details. “Other participants' proposals” opens the list of what the others propose or look for (only people with an active registration can see it, and you see your own in your section).

Your name next to a proposal appears **only if you agreed to show your attendance** (you manage it under “Consents”); without consent, or if you withdraw it, the proposal stays visible but reads “Author not shown”. If an organizer finds something in the text that must not be visible, they can hide it from the others: you still see and can edit it.
