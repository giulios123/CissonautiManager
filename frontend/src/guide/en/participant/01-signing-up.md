# Signing up for the camp

You sign up with the public form, without creating an account. It takes two minutes.

If you have already signed up, use the “Sign in” link above the form to open your personal page. When the form is embedded in the event website, sign-in opens in a new tab.

1. **Choose your shifts.** Every day has three slots: morning, afternoon and evening. Tap the tiles you are interested in, even on days that are not consecutive. “Whole day” and the buttons by time of day save you taps.
2. **Enter your details.** First name, last name, email, phone (required), who invited you (optional) and what you can bring to the camp.
3. **Consents.** The privacy notice and the liability waiver are required: you can read both before ticking.

While you choose, the summary (at the bottom on a phone) shows the fee you are putting together.

When you send the form you see the “Registration received” page with the amount, payment reference, payment instructions and deadline. You also get an email with the link to your page and, if the organizers set it up, a picture with your name that you can share.

### Important

- Your booking is confirmed **only after payment**: until then the places are held for a few days and then released.
- The fee is a fixed part plus an amount for every day you are here, even only in the evening.
- If a slot is full you will see it marked: you can join the waitlist (see the dedicated section).

A phone number is required, including when you select waitlisted slots only. You can include an international dialing code.
