# Managing optional consents

On your personal page you can choose separately whether to show your attendance in the members-only area and whether to consent to the use of photos and video. You can do this even if you are only on a waitlist or do not have an active booking.

Open the text for a purpose, choose “Yes” or “No”, and save. The choices start blank: leaving them unanswered is not the same as declining. If the text changes, a previous yes does not authorize the new version; read it and choose again. You can withdraw a yes at any time, including after an update.

The two choices are independent and do not change your place, fee, or payment. The history shows the version, date, and text associated with each choice. If the complete text is unavailable, you cannot grant a new yes; you can still withdraw a previous grant.
