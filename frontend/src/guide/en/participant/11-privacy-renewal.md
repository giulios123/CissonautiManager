# Review an updated privacy notice

When a new privacy notice is published, it appears on your personal page after you sign in. Read the text and select “I have read and accept the privacy notice” to edit attendance, meals, activities and contributions again.

Until you accept the new version, you can view your registration and payments, change your language or password, update optional and dietary consents, leave a waitlist, or cancel. You can review previous privacy notices in the same section.

You also receive an email asking you to sign in to your personal page. Its link opens the sign-in page; acceptance is recorded only after you read the notice and make your choice there.
