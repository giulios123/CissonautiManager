# Changing your slots

As long as the booking is awaiting payment or confirmed you can change it with “Change your slots”: add or remove slots and see the new fee right away, then save.

- At least one slot must remain. If you want to withdraw completely, use the cancellation.
- The hold deadline does not change.
- Slots on days that have already passed cannot be changed from here: write to the organizers if needed.
- Removing a slot also removes the meals and activities for times when you are no longer here: the app tells you after saving.
- Full slots cannot be added, but you can join the waitlist.
