# Changing your slots

Your personal page shows attendance in one row per day, after the status and balance. Full days show only the date; partial days list their booked periods. Evening attendance is included when you book both morning and afternoon.

As long as the booking is awaiting payment or confirmed you can edit it with “Edit attendance”: add or remove slots and see the new amounts right away, then save. With the new rule, the preview separates the registration fee (base plus days) from the total due to the organizers, which also includes extras.

- At least one slot must remain. If you want to withdraw completely, use the cancellation.
- The hold deadline does not change.
- Slots on days that have already passed cannot be changed from here: write to the organizers if needed.
- Removing a slot also removes the meals and activities for times when you are no longer here: the app tells you after saving.
- Full slots cannot be added, but you can join the waitlist.
