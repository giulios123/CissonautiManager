# Signing in to your page

There is no password to remember: on the “Sign in” page type your email, press “Send the link” and you get a link that opens your page. The link is valid for 10 minutes: open it and you are in, with no extra step. If you open it again within those 10 minutes you get in again.

You can also click the app logo to reach your page. If you are signed out, you will first see the “Sign in” page.

After signing in you stay signed in for 14 days on that device. When you are done you can press “Sign out”.

### I prefer a password

At the bottom of your page you can set a password (it is optional). Then, on the “Sign in” page, use “Or sign in with your password”. The email link always works, even if you forget the password.

### The link doesn't work

“Invalid link” means more than 10 minutes have passed. Press “Request a new link” and try again. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder.
