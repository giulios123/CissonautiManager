# Signing in to your page

There is no password to remember: on the “Sign in” page type your email, press “Send the link” and you get a link that opens your page. The link is valid for 30 minutes and works once.

After signing in you stay signed in for 14 days on that device. When you are done you can press “Sign out”.

### I prefer a password

At the bottom of your page you can set a password (it is optional). Then, on the “Sign in” page, use “Or sign in with your password”. The email link always works, even if you forget the password.

### The link doesn't work

“Invalid link” means it has expired or you have already used it. Press “Request a new link” and try again. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder.
