# Paying

Payments do not go through the app: you make them yourself with one of the listed channels (Revolut, Satispay, PayPal or bank transfer) and the organizers record them by hand.

1. On your page you find **fee**, **paid** and **still to pay**.
2. Make the payment following the instructions of the channel you prefer.
3. Write your personal code as the **payment reference** (like `CSC-7K3P`): it is always shown above the instructions. It is only used to recognize the payment, not to sign in.
4. When recorded payments reach the amount required, the status changes from “Awaiting payment” to “Confirmed” and you get an email.

If the edition uses the new rule, you only need to cover the **registration fee** to confirm your place: the base fee plus each distinct selected day. Lunches, dinners and activities do not count toward confirmation. Your page separates the fee, amount still needed to confirm, total due, payments and overall balance; extras can be paid later.

### The deadline

While the booking is awaiting payment, you have a few days to cover the amount needed to confirm, shown on the page. Halfway through and 24 hours before the deadline you get a reminder. If you don't pay in time the booking becomes “Expired” and the places are released. An already confirmed booking has no deadline for any extras still to pay.

### If you change something

Changing slots, meals or activities recalculates the amount. If you have already paid, you see the difference to pay or a credit. A confirmed booking stays confirmed.

The camp fee and balance cover amounts collected by the organisation. Activities or meals paid to a provider on site show their cost and instructions separately; these costs do not increase the camp balance. A meal covered by the registration fee still counts as a sign-up and adds no further amount due. The app does not track whether you have paid the provider.

If the organisers have **exempted** the fee or an item (for example because you are a speaker), you see it marked “Exempt” and it does not count towards the total: the other items are still due. If you paid more than was due, the organisers can **refund your credit**: the refund appears in the list of recorded payments, your place does not change and the balance is already up to date.
