# Activities

Here you create the camp's activities: title, description, **day**, **time slot** (morning, afternoon, evening), start and end times, price (even zero) and capacity (optional). Participants see them on their page and sign up if they are present in that slot.

Once someone has signed up:

- you cannot change day and time slot,
- capacity cannot go below the number of people signed up,
- the activity cannot be deleted.

Before that you can delete it and it disappears from the participants' page. Tapping an activity shows who has signed up and the places. From a person's page you can choose activities on their behalf, and only there can you exceed capacity with “Force over capacity”.

“Download CSV” exports the activities and who signed up.

Choose start and end times using hour/minute menus within the selected slot (Rome time); evening activities may end at midnight. Select whether the organizers or the provider collects payment. Direct payment requires a cost and instructions including the recipient; that cost is excluded from the camp fee. Cost and recipient can change after sign-ups: saving recalculates affected active bookings while retaining recorded payments and showing any credit.
