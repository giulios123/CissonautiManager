# Guests

Here you add people without an account who take part in meals and activities: companions, guests, speakers who do not register. For each one you enter first and last name and choose whether they are **paying** or **not paying**; phone, reference (for example “comes with Maria”) and internal note are optional.

A guest has no account, email, booking or coworking slot: they only get what you assign.

- **Meals**: choose any lunch or dinner on the days of the edition, past days included, without needing a presence. This rule applies to guests only.
- **Activities**: the guest takes a seat of the capacity. If the activity is full you can still add them by ticking “Force over capacity”; participants see the activity as full but not who holds the seats.
- **Diet and allergies**: the diet is always saved. Allergies are health data: they are saved only by ticking “I collected the person's consent”; clearing the tick erases them. Only you and the other organisers see them, never in emails or in the guests CSV.

If you add someone with the same name as another guest or as a participant with an active booking, the app asks you to confirm: save only if it is a different person.

In this version guests generate no amounts: they do not appear in “To collect” or in the accounts. They do appear in **Meals**, **Week**, **Activities** and the **Report**, always as separate people (a person counts once per meal or activity). Deleting a guest removes their meals and activities too; the action stays in the history.
