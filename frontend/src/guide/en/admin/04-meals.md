# Meals

For every day and every meal (lunch and dinner) you see how many people eat, who they are and how they eat: the **diets** (omnivore, vegetarian, vegan) and the **allergies and intolerances** written with the health consent.

Allergies are health data: they stay in this view, on the registration page and in the meals CSV, and never appear in emails or in the report (there only the number appears). Handle them with the care they need and don't pass the CSV to people who don't need it.

Lunch and dinner prices are changed in Settings.

Use “Configure payment” for each date and meal to choose who collects payment. For direct payment, enter the specific cost and instructions including the recipient. Sign-ups and dietary needs stay in the app. Switching back to the organizers restores the standard meal price. Saving recalculates affected active bookings: recorded payments remain, overpayments become credit, and confirmed bookings stay confirmed. CSV exports distinguish cost, recipient and instructions.
