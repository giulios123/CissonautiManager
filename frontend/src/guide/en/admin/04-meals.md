# Meals

For every day and every meal (lunch and dinner) you see how many people eat, who they are and how they eat: the **diets** (omnivore, vegetarian, vegan) and the **allergies and intolerances** written with the health consent.

Allergies are health data: they stay in this view, on the registration page and in the meals CSV, and never appear in emails or in the report (there only the number appears). Handle them with the care they need and don't pass the CSV to people who don't need it.

Lunch and dinner prices are changed in Settings.

Use “Configure payment” for each date and meal to choose who collects payment. For direct payment, enter the specific cost and instructions including the recipient. Sign-ups and dietary needs stay in the app. A meal covered by the fee still counts as a sign-up and has zero due; the CSV shows its coverage and due amount. Switching back to the organisers restores the standard meal price. Saving recalculates affected active bookings: recorded payments remain, overpayments become credit, and confirmed bookings stay confirmed. CSV exports distinguish collection by the organisation, on-site provider payment and meals covered by the fee.

The list also includes **guests**, labelled “Guest” and linking to their page: their account is on the guest's page. The CSV has a “Type” column (Participant or Guest).

## The order of the days

During the edition the days are in a “useful” order: **today** first (marked “Today”), then the days to come, then the past ones, each group in chronological order. Before and after the edition the order is chronological. The day is the one in Rome, whatever the timezone of your phone.

The order updates when you open or reload the page and when you come back to the browser tab after leaving it, so the new day moves to the top on its own. If a window is open, a field is being edited or an editor is open, nothing reloads and nothing moves: it tries again the next time you return. “Download CSV” always stays in chronological order.
