# Form text and other emails

In **Settings**, below “Registration email”, you find the rest of the texts that participants read. An edition must already be saved. The **Italian** or **English** tab at the top chooses the language you are editing.

### The text above the form

“Text above the form” is the paragraph shown under the title of the sign-up page. You can write several lines and the line breaks stay. The box with the hold days and the three steps fills itself in. If you leave it empty, the standard text is used (you see it in grey in the field).

### The other emails

For each one (waitlist, already registered, sign-in link, payment received, the two reminders, place opened up, cancellation) open its card and edit:

- **Subject**.
- **Opening text**: the greeting and the first sentence.

After the opening text the booking data always follows (slots, fee, payment reference, deadline, payment instructions, link, and the closing courtesy lines): the card lists this **automatic part**, which cannot be edited.

### Placeholders

In the texts you can write `{first_name}`, `{last_name}`, `{event}`, `{start_date}` and `{end_date}`: they become the first name, last name, edition name and dates. A wrong placeholder is refused when you save. The standard subjects use `{event}`: they follow the edition name. If you write your own subject without `{event}`, the edition name does not appear and does not change with Settings.

### Restoring

A field left empty uses the standard text: to go back, empty it and save. Changes apply to the emails sent from then on, not to those already sent.
