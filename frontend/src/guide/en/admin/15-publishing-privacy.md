# Publish a new privacy notice

In **Settings**, under the privacy notice, enter the full text in Italian and English. Each publication creates a new version. Acceptance becomes pending for people with an active booking or an open waitlist entry, and the request email is sent by the next hourly job.

People can still view amounts and payments, manage consents, leave a waitlist and cancel. Changes to attendance, new waitlist entries, meals, activities and contributions stay paused until they accept the notice on their personal page. The participant’s saved language determines the email language.

Previous versions and acceptance records remain available in the person’s consent history. Review the final legal text before publishing it for the live edition.
