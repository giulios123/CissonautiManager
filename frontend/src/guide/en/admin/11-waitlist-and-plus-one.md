# Waitlist and +1

### How it works

When a slot is full people join the waitlist. The list has a cap (capacity times percentage, adjustable) and there is none for the evening. When a place frees up, because of a cancellation, a slot removed, more capacity or an expiry, the first person on the list gets the slot and an email. After an expiry the handover happens with the hourly job, so within an hour.

### The +1

Someone who writes “who invited you?” on the form can be a +1. You confirm the link with the “Verified +1” tick, from the registration page or from “Days and slots”. A verified +1 goes **ahead of** the others on the list, but not beyond the cap: with a full list not even they get in. Among several +1 the order of arrival applies.

- If “invited by” is emptied, the verification falls.
- Verifying after the person is already on the list takes effect right away, because the order is recalculated every time.
- People who move down in position are not notified.
