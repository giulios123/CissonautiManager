# Deliveries

Here you record the **benefits** of the edition (the bottle, the t-shirt…) as you hand them out. The catalogue of benefits is filled in Settings; with no benefit in the catalogue the page says so and shows nothing.

## Who is entitled

Someone with an **active** registration (waiting for payment or confirmed) and at least one of these:

- they are **internal and not paying** (for example a speaker);
- their **fee is exempted**, even if the category stays “ordinary”;
- they paid, net of refunds, at least the **base fee** (not the one with the days). If the base fee is zero, this condition alone is not enough.

Whoever is only on the waiting list, or whose registration expired, was cancelled or refunded, and external guests, are not entitled. This threshold is not the one that confirms the place, which depends on the base fee and the days.

## How to use it

Choose the benefit (next to the name you see how many you handed out and how many people are entitled), search by name and, if needed, filter on “To deliver” or “Delivered”. Press **“Deliver”** on the row: who, when and for which benefit stay on record. A double click or two organisers at once do not make two deliveries: there is only one active delivery for each person and benefit.

If you made a mistake, press **“Annul delivery…”** and write the reason (required). The delivery does not disappear: it stays in the history, annulled, with who annulled it and why, and the person goes back to “To deliver”. “Download CSV” exports the list with the same filters.

## If the person loses the entitlement

If after the delivery the person cancels, changes category or the payment is refunded, the delivery **stays** and the row is marked “No longer entitled”. It is not deleted and no return is recorded. If they get the entitlement back, the mark disappears.

When a benefit is retired from the catalogue no new deliveries can be recorded, but the history stays readable and can be annulled. The catalogue and the deliveries do not carry over to the next edition by themselves. Participants see none of this.
