# Days and slots

For every day and every slot (morning, afternoon, evening) it shows how many people there are, how many places are left and who is here, with names that lead to their page.

- The evening has no limit on places. For people who booked morning and afternoon, evening presence is **derived**, and the view marks it.
- Full slots also show who is on the **waitlist**, in the order they would be promoted.
- The **+1** mark shows who said they were invited and has the link confirmed (see “Waitlist and +1”).
- “Download CSV” exports the view.

If you see no days, the edition is not set up yet: go to Settings.

## The order of the days

During the edition the days are in a “useful” order: **today** first (marked “Today”), then the days to come, then the past ones, each group in chronological order. Before and after the edition the order is chronological. The day is the one in Rome, whatever the timezone of your phone.

The order updates when you open or reload the page and when you come back to the browser tab after leaving it, so the new day moves to the top on its own. If a window is open, a field is being edited or an editor is open, nothing reloads and nothing moves: it tries again the next time you return. “Download CSV” always stays in chronological order.
