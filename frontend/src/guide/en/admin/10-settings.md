# Settings

Everything that defines the edition, in sections.

- **Edition**: name (it appears in the header, in the browser tab title and in the email subjects), dates, slot capacity, days a place is held, waitlist percentage, base fee and per-day fee, lunch and dinner prices. Save with “Save edition”.
- **Logo**: upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 5 MB to customize the header. You can replace it or restore the original logo.
- **Slot times**: start and end of morning, afternoon and evening.
- **Payment channels**: the instructions participants see for each channel (Revolut, Satispay, PayPal, bank transfer). Save with “Save channels”.
- **Registration email**: text and picture of the confirmation email, in Italian and English (see the dedicated section).
- **Text above the form and other emails**: the words of the sign-up form and of the other emails, per language (see the dedicated section).
- **Legal texts**: privacy notice, liability waiver and consent for allergies and intolerances. Every change with “Publish new version” creates a new version: people who already signed up keep the one they accepted.
- **Confirmation rule**: calculate a preview to see fees, totals, proposed statuses, balances or credits, and expected emails. If you customized email texts, the preview lists them for review; it does not change them. Review them before applying the rule, because new confirmation emails are sent immediately; if you edit a text, calculate a fresh preview. Current prices may change balances, but recorded payments are untouched. Only “Apply the new rule” performs the transition; expired and closed bookings are excluded. If data changes, refresh the preview before applying it.

It is better to fix the prices before opening registrations: once people have signed up, for example, the price of an activity can no longer be changed.

If the edition is not saved, channels and legal texts cannot be configured yet.
