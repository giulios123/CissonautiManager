# Payments to review

The list of registrations that need a look because of money, with the reason next to them:

- expired with recorded payments,
- confirmed with a difference to pay,
- confirmed with a credit (overpaid),
- awaiting payment with a partial payment,
- cancellation requested to refund, or without payments to close.

By opening the page you record the missing payment, the refund or the closing. A row disappears by itself when the situation is back in order. If the view is empty: “All in order”.

Under the new rule, a confirmed booking stays covered by payments for the base fee plus selected days, not by extras. If you void a payment and the fee is no longer covered, the booking goes back to awaiting payment; a positive extra balance on a confirmed booking remains to collect.

The normal wait of someone who has not paid yet does not appear here: to check the account and mark payments there is the “To collect” page.
