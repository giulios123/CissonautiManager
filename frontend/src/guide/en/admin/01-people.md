# People and the registration page

This is the starting page of the panel: one row per person, with code, status, days, evenings, paid, due, balance and deadline. With the new rule, the table also shows the registration fee and amount remaining to confirm; the registration page shows the overall balance or credit. With the search field you find someone by name, email or code, with the filter you choose the status, and with “Download CSV” you export the list (it opens in Excel). CSV files come out in the language the app is in at that moment (headers and statuses), with the same format as always.

Open “Cancellation history” to find new cancellations for the edition, including after a booking was removed. Search by name or email to see who requested the cancellation, when it happened, the edition, and the outcome.

Tapping a person opens their **registration page**.

When you sign in (with the link or the password) you land on your own personal page, like any registered person, with the “Go to the organizers' panel” button at the top. From the panel, “My page” takes you back there.

### What you see

Details and consents, attendance in one row per day (with times and partial periods), meals with dietary needs (allergies included), activities, waitlist and the **history** of the changes you made by hand (who, when, what).

### Recording a payment

Under “Record a payment” you enter amount, channel, date and a note. Under the new rule, a pending booking offers the amount remaining to confirm; the place is confirmed when payments cover the base fee plus selected days. Payments stay recorded in full and extras remain in the balance. A wrong payment is not deleted: you void it by writing the reason. It stays in the list, struck through, and no longer counts in the balance. If a void leaves the fee uncovered, the booking goes back to awaiting payment with a new hold.

### Actions on the registration

- **Edit details**: name, contacts, “invited by”, what they bring.
- **Change slots**: even for days already over. To add a slot that is full you must tick “Force over capacity”. The same tick is needed to sign someone up for an activity that is already full.
- **Extend the hold**: moves the deadline of a booking awaiting payment.
- **Reactivate the booking**: if it has expired, puts the places on hold again for the usual days, as long as there is still room in all the slots. If a payment already recorded covers the fee, it confirms the booking right away.
- **Cancel on the person's behalf**: the places are released right away. Without payments the booking is removed.
- **Mark as refunded**: for a cancellation with payments. The amount cannot exceed what was paid. The app does not make the refund and does not write to the person: you do that outside the system.

Remember that to give someone a meal or an activity you first need the right slots.

# Optional consents

On a person's details page, you can read the history of optional consents. This is read-only: an organizer cannot grant or withdraw a choice on someone's behalf. The history is also available for people who only appear on waitlists.

# Category, exemptions and refunds of credit

On the page of a pending or confirmed booking you find **Category and exemptions**.

- **Category**: “ordinary” or “internal, not paying” (for example a speaker). It is only a label, visible to you, in the People list (with a filter) and in the CSV: it **changes no amount** and the participant does not see it.
- **Exemptions**: you can exempt the **participation fee** and/or single **meals** and **activities** (the ones the organisation collects). An exempt item is worth zero; the others stay due. If you exempt the meal covered by the fee, the coverage moves to the next meal.
- **Preview and apply**: press “Show the preview” to see fee, total, paid, balance and status before and after; only then can you “Apply”. If the booking changes in the meantime, the app asks you to preview again. Payments are never touched. A pending booking that ends up covered becomes confirmed (without inventing a payment) and the person gets the usual email; a confirmed one stays confirmed even if you remove an exemption and a balance appears.
- If the person removes an exempt meal or activity and then chooses it again, the exemption is lost.

**Credit and refunds.** If they paid more than the total due, the page shows the **credit** and “Record a refund”: amount (never above the credit), channel, date and note. A refund does not change the place, does not delete payments and sends no email; if you press twice it is recorded once. A wrong one is voided with a reason and stays in the list, struck through. Balance and confirmation use the **net paid** (payments minus refunds): a payment that a refund relies on is voided after voiding the refund. The refund that closes a cancellation stays as it was, capped at the net paid.
