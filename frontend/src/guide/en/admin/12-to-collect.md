# To collect

The page to check the account and mark payments one after another, without opening every page. It lists the registrations waiting for money: first those awaiting payment, from the nearest deadline, then the confirmed ones with a difference to pay, finally the expired ones (a bank transfer can arrive late).

## How to use it

1. At the top choose the **channel** the payments you are about to mark arrived through (Revolut, Satispay, PayPal, bank transfer). The choice applies to the whole page and the browser remembers it.
2. Look at the movement on the account and type the **payment reference** (for example CSC-GGSU), the name or the email in the search field: the list narrows down.
3. Press **Mark as paid** on the right row. A payment is recorded with today's date and the chosen channel. Under the new rule, pending or expired bookings use the amount remaining to confirm; confirmed bookings with extras still use the full balance.

## If you pick the wrong row

For **10 seconds** the row shows “Paid … · Undo”. Pressing Undo voids the payment and the registration goes back to awaiting payment, with a new hold. After the 10 seconds the row leaves the list.

The confirmation email to the person goes out **after 30 seconds**, so a timely undo keeps it from being sent. Once sent it cannot be withdrawn: if you need to void later, do it from the registration page and write to the person.

## An amount different from the balance

If a different amount arrived on the account, use “Other amount” to enter the amount, date, and note in a dialog. The balance updates after saving. If another admin changed it while you were working, review the new values and confirm again. Undo and the delayed email use the same timings as quick payment.

## An expired booking that paid

If you mark an expired registration as paid and there is still room, it is reactivated. If a slot has filled up in the meantime it stays expired and the page tells you: decide how to proceed with the person.

For unusual cases (credit, partial payment, cancellations) there is the “To review” page.
