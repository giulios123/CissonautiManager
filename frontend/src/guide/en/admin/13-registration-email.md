# Registration email

The email sent when someone signs up is customized in **Settings**, in the “Registration email” section. It has two tabs, **Italian** and **English**: each person gets the email in the language they chose when signing up.

### Text

- **Subject** and **opening text** are optional: if you leave them empty, the email uses the standard text (you see it in grey inside the field).
- In the text you can use **placeholders**, which become the person's details: first name, last name, event, start date and end date. The buttons under the text insert them where your cursor is.
- After your text the email always continues with the amounts and personal link. Under the legacy rule it shows the fee as before; under the new rule it separates the registration fee, total and amount still needed to confirm. If the fee is zero, the booking is already confirmed and the email omits the payment reference, instructions and deadline. This part can't be edited, so the operational details stay consistent.
- A wrong placeholder (for example `{name}`) is refused when you save, with the list of valid ones.

### Picture

- Upload a **background** (JPEG, PNG or WebP, 5 MB at most). The app resizes it and writes a sentence on it, for example “I'm Ada Lovelace and I'll be at Cissonauti Summer Camp from 10 July 2027 to 16 July 2027” (in Italian “Sono Ada Lovelace e sarò a …”).
- You can change the **sentence**, the **color** and the **position** of the text (top, middle, bottom). Placeholders work here too.
- The **preview** updates as you type, with the sample name “Ada Lovelace”.
- Without a background the email goes out without a picture. “Remove the background” goes back to the text-only email.
- People who only join a waitlist don't get the picture.

### Try it before using it

“Send me a test” sends the email, with the saved texts, to your address and with your name, in the language of the open tab. Save before testing.
