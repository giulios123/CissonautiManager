# Edition report

The summary of the numbers: registrations, attendance, meals, activities, contributions, waitlist and income. Where it matters, **confirmed** people and those **awaiting payment** are kept apart, so you know what is certain and what is not.

- Allergies appear only as a number.
- The amount due is broken down into fee, meals and activities; a “difference” row absorbs prices changed after sign-ups.
- For the waitlist you also see how many of those promoted were verified +1 at the time of promotion.
- It can be **printed** (the menu and buttons disappear, about 4 A4 pages) and **downloaded as CSV**.

The numbers match the other views: if something doesn't add up, first check that the registrations are up to date.
