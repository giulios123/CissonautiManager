# Edition report

The summary of the numbers: registrations, attendance, meals, activities, contributions, waitlist and income. Where it matters, **confirmed** people and those **awaiting payment** are kept apart, so you know what is certain and what is not.

- Allergies appear only as a number.
- The amount due is broken down into fee, meals and activities; a “difference” row absorbs prices changed after sign-ups.
- “Still to collect” is the full balance; “Remaining to confirm” is only the uncovered registration fee for bookings awaiting confirmation. They describe different amounts and are not added together.
- For the waitlist you also see how many of those promoted were verified +1 at the time of promotion.
- It can be **printed** (the menu and buttons disappear, about 4 A4 pages) and **downloaded as CSV**.

The numbers match the other views: if something doesn't add up, first check that the registrations are up to date.

Sign-ups paid directly to a provider count toward attendance but are excluded from organizers’ expected revenue and amounts due. The activity report identifies who collects payment; payment details appear in the meal and activity views and CSV exports.

In the **Meals** and **Activities** sections an “Of which guests” column shows how many of the totals are guests; the total includes them. Presences, registrations and fees do not change.

The **Guest payments** table shows what guests owe, paid, were refunded, still have to pay and their credits, apart from the participants' fees. The **Event total** is the net of the fees plus the net of the guests; the same amount never appears in both. Costs paid to the provider are in neither.
