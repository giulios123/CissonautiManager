# Week

A glance at the whole edition: for every day the attendance in the morning, afternoon and evening, the lunches, the dinners and the activities. At the bottom is the total of different people. It is mainly useful to organize spaces and the kitchen.

“Download CSV” exports the view.

Lunches, dinners and activities also include **guests**; coworking presences stay those of the participants.

## What is behind a number

Every number in the table, totals included, is a button: press it (or reach it with Tab and press Enter) and the list that explains exactly that count opens, with a link to each person's page.

- **Morning, afternoon, evening, people:** the participants who are present. An evening implied by the whole day is marked “derived evening”.
- **Lunches and dinners:** participants and guests (guests are marked “external”, with a link to their page).
- **Activities:** one line per sign-up, with the activity title. At the top you see “sign-ups · different people”: someone signed up to two activities counts as two sign-ups but one person.
- **Zero:** the list is empty and says so.

The list shows no email, phone, dietary needs, allergies or amounts. If someone has signed up or changed their days in the meantime and the number no longer matches the table, the window says so and the table refreshes. Esc or “Close” bring the cursor back to the number you started from.
