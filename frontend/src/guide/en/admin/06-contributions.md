# Contributions

The list of what participants propose to bring or look for: talks, topics for the unconference, skills offered and skills wanted, with title, description and who wrote them. The “Type” filter narrows the list.

Contributions from bookings that are no longer active stay visible, marked as such. “Download CSV” exports the view.

## Hiding them from the other participants

Participants with an active registration can read the others' proposals; the author's name appears only if they consented to showing their attendance, otherwise the proposal is anonymous. Free text may still contain names: if needed, press **“Hide from the others”** (with an optional reason) and the proposal disappears from the others' list. It is not deleted or changed, the author still sees and can edit it, and the “Visibility to others” column and the CSV mark it as hidden. “Show again” puts it back. Every choice stays in the history of the participant's page.

## Turning them into activities

A proposal can become one or more activities. On its row press **“Create activity”**: the form opens with the proposal's title and description in the language you are using; the other language stays empty, to be completed (no translation is made up). Choose day, part of the day, times, cost and capacity and save: until you save, no activity exists, and “Cancel” closes without leaving anything behind.

After saving, the “Activities” column lists the activities created from the proposal, with a link to each. You can press “Create activity” again to create another one from the same proposal. A double click on save does not create two activities; opening the form twice and saving twice creates two.

The activities stay independent: if you edit or hide the proposal, or the author deletes it, they do not change. The author is not signed up to the activity. If the proposal goes away, the activity page shows “From the proposal “…” (no longer available)”: the original title stays, visible to the organisers only. The contributions CSV has an “Activities created” column.
